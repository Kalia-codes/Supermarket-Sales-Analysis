# Supermarket Sales Analysis

## 1. Project Objective

The aim of this project is to analyze supermarket sales data and find useful
information about products, branches, categories, customers, payment methods,
and customer ratings.

---

## 2. Dataset

The dataset contains 500 sales transactions with the following information:

- Product
- Category
- Branch
- City
- Customer Type
- Quantity
- Unit Price
- Payment Method
- Rating
- Sales

Sales are calculated using:

Sales = Quantity × Unit Price

## 11. Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following actions are recommended:

### 1. Maintain stock of high-selling products

Cheese generated the highest product sales, so its stock should be
monitored carefully to avoid shortages.

### 2. Focus on high-performing categories

Beverages generated the highest category sales. The supermarket can
maintain sufficient inventory and consider promotions for popular
beverage products.

### 3. Study Branch B performance

Branch B (Delhi) generated the highest sales in the calculated dataset.
Its product mix, customer behavior, and sales strategies can be studied
for possible application to other branches.

### 4. Support digital payments

Credit Card was the most frequently used payment method, followed by
Debit Card and UPI. The supermarket should continue supporting multiple
digital payment options.

### 5. Improve membership engagement

Normal customers had a slightly higher average transaction value than
members. Membership benefits and offers can be reviewed to encourage
higher spending among members.

### 6. Improve customer experience

The average rating was 3.99/5. Customer feedback can be analyzed further
to identify opportunities to improve service quality.

This analysis shows how supermarket sales data can be converted into

The analysis identified the highest-selling product, highest-selling
category, best-performing branch, popular payment methods, customer

These insights can help supermarket management improve inventory
planning, branch performance, payment support, customer engagement,
and overall business decisions.
